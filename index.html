Lead triage in the CRM

New prospects arrived unsorted, so deciding who to chase first depended on whoever read them.

I automated categorization in Lawmatics against a five-category disposition framework: chase hard, chase, refer out, reject and inconclusive.

A morning sweep for referred clients

Clients referred by other attorneys and past clients are the warmest leads, and they were easy to miss in a busy inbox.

A scheduled sweep runs every weekday morning, flags each referred lead for priority follow-up and adds the referral source to a running log. Alongside it, automated voicemail triage and a daily schedule briefing keep nothing waiting.

›
AfterFlagged before the day starts, with a record of who to thank
